About

I build AI systems for people the mainstream tooling tends to leave out: offline-first apps for farmers on low-end phones, speech recognition for Ghanaian languages, and machine-learning surrogates that replace hours of physics simulation with millisecond inference.

My background spans a materials science degree with density functional theory (DFT) research and more than three years shipping production software across startups. I care most about work where the deployment context is the hard part: intermittent connectivity, constrained budgets, and languages and users that large models were never tuned for.
